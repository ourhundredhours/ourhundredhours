Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 August 2026

At Our Hundred Hours, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: Name and email address, only when voluntarily provided through our contact form.
  • Usage Data: Anonymous information about how you interact with our website, such as pages visited and time spent.
  • Technical Data: Browser type, device information, and IP address for security and analytics purposes.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and provide customer support
  • Improve our website and content
  • Monitor website security and prevent fraud
  • Understand how visitors engage with our content

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Your Rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. You may also object to or restrict certain processing activities. To exercise these rights, please contact us at hello@ourhundredhours.com.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
